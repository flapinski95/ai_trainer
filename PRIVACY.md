# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the **AI Endurance Coach** application ("the Application"). The Application is a private, non-commercial tool designed to process personal fitness and health data to provide customized training insights.

## 2. Information We Collect
When you connect your Garmin Connect account, we collect:
- **Activity Data:** Workout duration, distance, heart rate, power, speed, cadence, and GPS tracks.
- **Health & Wellness Data:** Daily sleep metrics, Heart Rate Variability (HRV) status, Body Battery, and resting heart rate (RHR).

## 3. How We Use Your Data
We use the collected data strictly to:
- Generate personalized, natural language training feedback and recovery recommendations.
- Display activity summaries and historical trends within the Application interface.

## 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services
- **Storage:** Access tokens and aggregated health/workout metrics are securely stored in encrypted cloud databases (AWS DynamoDB) using AWS KMS encryption.
- **AI Processing:** Workout summaries and health metrics are transmitted to Third-Party AI providers (Google AI Studio / Anthropic API) via secure API calls solely for the purpose of generating training feedback.
- **Data Sharing:** We **do not** sell, share, or monetize your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

## 5. Data Retention & Disconnection
You can disconnect your Garmin Connect account at any time within the Application settings. Disconnecting your account will immediately revoke access and delete stored OAuth tokens from our database.

## 6. Contact Us
For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub.
